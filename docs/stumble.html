No algorithm. Just people.

A stumble is random on purpose. There is no engagement ranking, no recommendation model and no AI-written review layer. The registry is a small public experiment in a web that people can map and discuss for themselves.

  • 👍 Upvote a destination you found useful.
  • 👎 Downvote a dead, unsafe or low-value site.
  • ✍ Comment on GitHub. Human-written only.
  • ⚑ Report abuse to the registry maintainers.
